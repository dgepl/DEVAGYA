# Phase 4 – 15 Specialized AI Agents Manifest

1. **Teacher Mentor AI** (`teacher_mentor`): Pedagogical coach for lesson advice and classroom management.
2. **Question Generator AI** (`question_generator`): Generates CBSE/ICSE exam question papers & Bloom's taxonomy questions.
3. **Lesson Planner AI** (`lesson_planner`): Crafts 45-minute daily & weekly unit lesson plans.
4. **Homework Assistant AI** (`homework_assistant`): Explains homework questions and verifies steps with guided examples.
5. **Socratic Student Tutor AI** (`student_tutor`): Interactive Socratic tutor guiding students step-by-step without spoiling answers.
6. **English & Communication Coach** (`english_coach`): Improves grammar, essay writing, and daily spoken fluency.
7. **Academic Research Assistant** (`research_assistant`): Summarizes research papers and extracts citations.
8. **Document AI Assistant** (`document_assistant`): Processes uploaded PDFs, textbooks, and worksheets into summaries, flashcards, and quizzes.
9. **Analytics & Performance AI** (`analytics_assistant`): Analyzes class marks, weak topic distributions, and student metrics.
10. **AI Parenting & Study Coach** (`parent_coach`): Suppiles evidence-based advice for parents to support home study.
11. **Career & Stream Counselor** (`career_counselor`): Guides subject stream choices (PCM/PCB/Commerce/Humanities) and college roadmaps.
12. **Revision & Mindmap Assistant** (`revision_assistant`): Generates 1-Day / 7-Day revision cheat sheets, formula lists, and mind maps.
13. **Exam Preparation Strategist** (`exam_strategist`): Creates board exam time allocation strategies and mock test plans.
14. **Growth Mindset Coach** (`motivation_coach`): Provides positive reinforcement, overcomes study burnout, and maintains streaks.
15. **AI Study Schedule Planner** (`study_planner`): Calculates realistic study timetables based on weak subjects.
