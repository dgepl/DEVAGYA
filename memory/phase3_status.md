# Phase 3 – Status & Memory Record

- **Phase**: Phase 3 (Student & Parent AI Learning Platform)
- **Completion Date**: July 2026
- **Status**: Completed & Production Ready

## Completed Features
1. **Student Dashboard**: Dynamic study plan, homework checklist, streak flame counter, XP meter, level progress, quick action tiles.
2. **Socratic AI Tutor**: Active recall mode toggle, hint suggestions, step-by-step guidance without direct answer spoiling, image/PDF upload support.
3. **Practice Mode**: Adaptive quiz runner supporting 9 question formats (MCQs, Short/Long, Case Studies, Assertion & Reason, Fill-in-blanks, Matching, True/False) with instant solution evaluation and XP earnings.
4. **Flashcards**: 3D flip card player, spaced repetition rating buttons (Again, Hard, Good, Easy), bookmarking, shuffle deck.
5. **Study Planner**: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, and Exam auto-generation based on weak topics and study hours.
6. **Revision Studio**: Quick Notes, Mind Maps, Formula Sheets, Cheat Sheets, 1-Day & 7-Day Revision Plans.
7. **AI Exam Prep**: Readiness index, weightage, expected questions, day-by-day countdown plan.
8. **Notion Notes**: Markdown editor with AI Summarization, AI Rewriting, and AI Quiz from Notes.
9. **Gamification**: XP points, daily streak, levels, badges, and school/class leaderboards with privacy opt-out.
10. **Pomodoro Focus Timer**: Focus/break timer, ambient focus music player, and XP session logging.
11. **Parent Portal**: Child overview dashboard, multi-child selector, homework tracking, attendance, teacher feedback.
12. **AI Parenting Coach**: Evidence-based parenting advice, actionable tips, discussion starters.
13. **Child Analytics**: Visual study time trends, subject mastery bars, weak/strong topic radar.
14. **Notifications**: Real-time alert feeds for parents and students.
15. **Database Extensions**: `supabase/schema_phase3.sql` schema DDL.
16. **Documentation System**: `/docs` and `/memory` synchronized documentation.
